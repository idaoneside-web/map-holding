Gradients & contrast pairs

Sections I · J

Motion

Restrained and purposeful: controlled fade-and-rise reveals, gentle image scale, short transitions on the standard easing curve. Bounce and overshoot are forbidden, and prefers-reduced-motion is honoured.

Staggered reveal · 700ms · ease-entrance · ≤80ms stagger
